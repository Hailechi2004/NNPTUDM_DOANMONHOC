Kien truc he thong website quan ly va ban phu tung o to

So do nay minh hoa kien truc thuc te cua project: nguoi dung thao tac tren giao dien EJS/HTML, request di qua Express Router, xu ly nghiep vu tai Controller va Utility, sau do truy van MySQL. He thong co phan quyen theo vai tro Admin, Staff, Customer va ho tro ca khu vuc quan tri lan shop online.

Tang nguoi dung Tang web server Tang nghiep vu Tang du lieu
Tang 1
Client Layer

Nguoi dung va giao dien

Day la diem bat dau cua he thong, gom ca nguoi quan tri noi bo va khach hang mua hang.

  • Admin: quan ly user, dashboard
  • Staff: quan ly phu tung, don hang, danh muc
  • Customer: xem shop, gio hang, dat hang
  • View Engine: EJS render HTML tren server
  • Static Assets: anh upload, CSS, giao dien minh hoa
Security

Xac thuc va phan quyen

  • JWT luu trong cookie
  • Middleware gan thong tin nguoi dung
  • Phan quyen theo Admin, Staff, Customer
  • Chan truy cap trai vai tro
Tang 2
Web Layer

Express Application

Server trung tam tiep nhan request, parse du lieu, goi route va render response cho giao dien.

  • app.js cau hinh toan bo ung dung
  • Middleware: logger, json, form, cookie
  • Static route cho /uploads va resources/static
  • Error handler xu ly loi tap trung
Routing

Cac nhom route chinh

  • /auth: dang nhap, dang ky, dang xuat
  • /parts: quan ly phu tung
  • /orders: quan ly don hang
  • /categories, /suppliers, /promotions
  • /shop: khu vuc mua hang
Tang 3
Business Layer

Controller xu ly nghiep vu

Moi controller phu trach mot module chuc nang va chua logic nghiep vu cua he thong.

  • Auth Controller
  • Parts Controller
  • Orders Controller
  • Shop Controller
  • Dashboard Controller
  • Categories, Suppliers, Promotions, Users
Nghiep vu noi bat

Xu ly mua hang

  • Tim kiem va loc phu tung
  • Them vao gio hang
  • Ap ma khuyen mai theo san pham
  • Checkout va tao don hang
  • Cap nhat ton kho sau dat hang
Tang 4
Utility Layer

Module ho tro

Day la tang dung chung cho xac thuc, ket noi CSDL, validate du lieu va upload file.

  • db.js: ket noi MySQL
  • authHandler.js: middleware dang nhap
  • validationHandler.js: kiem tra du lieu
  • uploadHandler.js: upload anh bang Multer
  • dbMigration.js: bo sung schema khi can
Data Processing

Quy tac xu ly

  • Kiem tra tinh hop le du lieu dau vao
  • Phan tich vai tro nguoi dung
  • Thuc hien transaction khi checkout
  • Render giao dien hoac tra loi loi
Tang 5
Database Layer

MySQL Database

Co so du lieu quan he luu toan bo du lieu van hanh cua he thong ban phu tung.

  • Users, Roles, Customers
  • Categories, Suppliers, Parts, Promotions
  • Carts, Cart Items
  • Orders, Order Details
  • Reviews
Data Output

Du lieu phan hoi

  • Trang giao dien EJS da render
  • Danh sach san pham va chi tiet san pham
  • Thong tin gio hang, don hang
  • Thong ke dashboard
Buoc 1

Nguoi dung truy cap he thong qua trinh duyet va gui yeu cau den website.

Buoc 2

Express nhan request, middleware kiem tra cookie, du lieu form va quyen truy cap.

Buoc 3

Router chuyen huong request den controller phu hop voi tung module chuc nang.

Buoc 4

Controller xu ly nghiep vu, ket hop cac utility de validate, upload va tinh toan.

Buoc 5

He thong truy van MySQL de doc, ghi, cap nhat thong tin san pham, gio hang va don hang.

Buoc 6

Ket qua duoc render thanh giao dien EJS de tra ve cho nguoi dung tren trinh duyet.

Goi y dua vao bao cao: Ban co the chup man hinh trang nay va dat ten hinh la "So do kien truc he thong website quan ly va ban phu tung o to". Neu can, co the mo ta ngan gon: he thong duoc xay dung theo kien truc web nhieu tang, trong do giao dien nguoi dung gui yeu cau den Express server, server xu ly thong qua route, controller va utility, sau do truy van co so du lieu MySQL va tra ket qua ve giao dien.
